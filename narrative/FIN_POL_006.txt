SECTION 1  Approvals
Audience: Finance
Classification: Internal

The group approvals policy applies to all payments, credits and commitments made by the company, and it sets the minimum level of authority for each transaction. Every approval must be recorded in Ledger with the name of the approver, the date and the purpose. No one may approve their own request, and the system blocks any attempt to do so. The authority matrix is signed by the finance director and reviewed whenever the organisation changes. Where a request is split into smaller parts to stay under a limit, the approver must treat it as one request. Emergency approvals are allowed only where the delay would cause a real loss, and they are confirmed in writing on the next working day. The approvals log is reviewed by the controller at the close of each period, and any unusual pattern is investigated. Staff who are unsure about their level of authority must ask before acting, not after.

SECTION 2  Payments
Audience: Finance
Classification: Internal

The group payments policy sets the standard for how money leaves the company, from supplier payments to refunds and transfers. Every payment must be supported by an approved invoice or a signed instruction, and the reference must be recorded in Ledger before the transfer is released. Bank details are verified before any new payee is set up, using a call to a number already held on file. Payments are made in batches where possible, and each batch is reconciled to the bank statement before the next batch begins. Urgent payments outside the batch cycle need the approval of the finance director and a written reason. Payment files are generated by the system and are not edited by hand. Access to the payment system is restricted to named users, and each user's access is reviewed at the end of each period.

SECTION 3  Billing
Audience: Finance
Classification: Internal

The group billing policy sets the principles for bills issued to consumers and business customers. Bills are generated from the active contract records in Ledger, and the billing team reviews a sample of each run before it is released. A bill must show the charges clearly, including taxes and one off items, and must not contain estimates unless the customer has agreed to them in writing. Corrections are issued as credit notes that link to the original bill, and the original is never deleted. Billing disputes are logged in Relay and are not included in collection action while they are open. The billing team reports the number of disputes and their causes at the close of each period. Any change to the billing cycle is approved by the finance director and communicated to customers in advance. Staff must not change billing data by hand in the production system.

SECTION 4  Insurance claims
Audience: Legal
Classification: Internal

The group insurance claims policy sets how the company presents and tracks claims made under its insurance cover. Each claim must be supported by the evidence required in the policy, such as photographs, invoices and an incident description. The legal team submits claims to the insurer INS1 and records the submission date and the reference number. Any reply from the insurer is logged within the same working day and reviewed by the legal team before a response is sent. Settlements are accepted only in writing and only after the legal team has checked them against the claim record. Claims that are declined are reviewed to see whether the policy wording or the supporting evidence was the cause. The legal team keeps a register of open claims that is reviewed at the close of each period. Staff in other departments do not contact the insurer directly, and they refer any enquiry to the legal team.
