# Procedure: Human Resources

Document HR_PRO_032, version 3.7, the Company, internal use

## SECTION 1  Working hours and access

Audience: All staff
Classification: Internal

Working hours are set by the department manager within the limits of the staff policy, and each employee receives a written schedule before the first day of work. Changes to the schedule are agreed with the manager in advance, and the new schedule is entered into the HR system before it takes effect. Staff who arrive late or leave early must inform the manager as soon as they know.

Access cards are issued by the facilities team after the HR start process is complete, and each card is linked to the employee's approved working hours. Access outside those hours is granted only with the written approval of the manager. A lost card is reported at once so that it can be disabled, and a replacement is issued after the identity has been confirmed.

## SECTION 2  Training

Audience: Human Resources
Classification: Internal

Training is planned at the start of each year with the manager, and the plan is recorded in the HR system with the course name, the provider and the expected completion date. Mandatory courses on conduct, data protection and safety are scheduled first and cannot be replaced by optional courses. Employees who fall behind the plan are reminded by the HR team and supported by their manager.

Each course completion is recorded by the employee in the HR system with the certificate attached, and the HR team checks the records each period. Where a course is delivered by an external provider, the HR team confirms that the provider is approved before the booking is made. Training costs are approved under the delegation table, and the approval is kept with the course record.

## SECTION 3  Leaving

Audience: Human Resources
Classification: Internal

When an employee leaves, the HR team issues a leaving checklist that covers the return of equipment, the removal of system access and the handover of open work. The checklist is completed by the manager and the employee together, and each item is signed when it is done. The HR team does not close the file until every item has been confirmed.

The final payment, including any amounts due for unused leave, is calculated by the payroll team and checked by the HR manager before it is released. The leaver receives a written statement that explains each element of the payment. Exit interviews are offered to every leaver, and the answers are reviewed each period to identify common reasons for departure. The HR team shares the summary with the department manager, who uses it when planning recruitment.

## SECTION 4  Training, part 2

Audience: Human Resources
Classification: Internal

Development plans are agreed between the employee and the manager, and they set out the skills to be built over the next review period. The development plan is linked to the training plan, so that every planned skill has a course or a practical task attached. Progress is discussed at the regular review, and the plan is updated where the role changes.

The HR team gathers feedback about training quality from participants, and the results are used to choose providers at the next renewal. Poor results are discussed with the provider, and a course that does not meet the standard is removed from the approved list. The feedback form is short, so that employees can complete it on the day of the course. Results are summarised for each provider and kept with the approval record for the course.

## SECTION 5  Insurance claims

Audience: Legal
Classification: Internal

Insurance claims for employee equipment are handled by the legal team with the support of the HR team, and each claim starts with a written report of the incident. A device insurance claim must be reported to partner INS1 within 4 days of the incident. The report describes the circumstances, the device, its value and any witnesses, and it is signed by the employee and the manager.

The HR team does not give the insurer any medical or personal data beyond what the claim requires, and the legal team controls the release of all documents. The employee is kept informed of the progress of the claim, and the outcome is confirmed in writing when the insurer has decided. Where a claim is rejected, the legal team considers whether the employee has a right of appeal under the policy.
