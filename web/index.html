need-to-know

Ask as a department. See only what it may read.

Every section of every document, in English or in Greek, says who may read it. So one question can be answered for sales and refused for legal, and neither is shown a paragraph written for the other.

Looking for the local server.

Search runs the retrieval alone. Ask sends its excerpts to the model as well, and scores the answer when the question is a dev golden question asked as its own role. Slow motion plays the trace one stage at a time; with reduced motion set, nothing moves.

Changes as they land

    The build

    A change, as the watcher took it

    A file changes on disk and ntk ingest watch scans it, cuts and embeds again only what changed, and syncs the index; a role then asks until its query sees the change. These are recorded runs, each lane one change, timed from the moment the file was written.
